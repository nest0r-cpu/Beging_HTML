The Intersection of Photography and Anthropology

What is Photography Anthropology?

Photography anthropology is the study of how photography can document and analyze human behaviors, cultures, and social practices. It explores the ways in which images reflect societal values and cultural narratives.

Importance of Visual Documentation

Visual documentation through photography plays a critical role in anthropology by preserving cultural heritage and providing insights into the lives of individuals and communities.

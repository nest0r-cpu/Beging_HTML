The Shift to Digital Photography

The transition from film to digital photography has revolutionized the way we take and share photos. With smartphones and digital cameras, photography is more accessible than ever before, leading to an explosion in the number of photos taken each year.

Film photography is still beloved by many professionals and hobbyists alike, but digital continues to dominate the market. The ability to instantly edit and share photos has changed the landscape of the photography industry forever.
